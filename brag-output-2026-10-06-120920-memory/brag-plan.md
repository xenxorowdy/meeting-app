# Brag Plan: Kesami

20 seconds · 1920×1080 · 30 fps · polished · music and restrained UI sounds · no narration.

## Planning rubric
1. Kesami is a desktop meeting recorder with transcription, source-linked decisions, reviewed commitments, historical Ask AI and confirmed follow-through.
2. The strongest idea: the meeting ends; the work doesn't. Remember what you promised.
3. Visual hook: a finished conversation fades while one promise survives.
4. Product: dark meeting detail, Transcript/Summary/Commitments/Actions tabs, exact-quote commitment review, citation cards and reviewed local draft.
5. Twenty seconds is the shortest satisfying version of the requested six beats.
6. Polished: quiet premium desktop product film; large typography, purposeful camera moves, restrained coral accents.
7. Steady understated electronic music, soft card arrivals and dry clicks. No voice.
8. Caption: The meeting ends. The work doesn't. Kesami remembers what you promised—and helps you follow through.
9. End meeting → review sourced commitment → ask across history → review a follow-up draft.

## Source and claim boundaries
Read current website/app/globals.css, website/lib/demo.ts, website/components/Logo.tsx, Memory.tsx and Commitments.tsx; apps/ui/src/design.css, components/brand/Logo.jsx, MeetingCommitments.jsx, MeetingActions.jsx, MeetingChatPanel.jsx and design/MeetingDetail.jsx. Use a source-derived motion reconstruction, not a live customer recording. All content is fictional Acme demo data. No credentials, real meetings or private user data.

Three open commitments refers to three uncompleted candidates in the featured meeting, not a shipped global commitment dashboard. The local task option stands in for the requested reminder: Kesami does not currently send reminder notifications. Drafts stay local; Calendar requires review/confirmation. Relationship chips represent typed source references, not an autonomous knowledge graph. Windows is a configured supported distribution platform; the film does not claim capture parity.

## Visual identity
Site canvas #f5f5f0, ink #111111, orange #ff5e00; desktop backgrounds #1c1c1e/#171719/#232326, line #45454c, text #e0e0e0/#b0b0b6, coral #e8634e. Inter from a local font asset, real four-bar mark geometry. App panels enlarged for video readability; no generic AI graphics, robots or stock footage.

## Storyboard
1. 0.00–3.20 — Problem. A meeting-ended window and fading conversation/notes/decision cues. “The meeting ends.” enters immediately; “The work doesn't.” follows after a pause. A small Kesami meeting-memory identity makes the category clear. One promise remains.
2. 3.20–5.80 — Product reveal. Actual-source dark desktop chrome, Acme customer discussion, transcript containing the Friday pricing promise. Brief progress and saved-summary state. Smooth upward reveal, quiet click at processing completion.
3. 5.80–8.60 — Memory. Decision, Commitment and Action emerge from the same meeting. Source quote remains visible. A restrained Meeting → Person → Company → Project → Commitment reference chain appears, with source-backed synthetic labels. Same promise card persists into the hero.
4. 8.60–12.30 — Commitment hero. Zoom into the persistent commitment card. “You committed to”, “Send Acme the pricing proposal”, “Due Friday”. The quote and Transcript · You · 32:10 remain. “3 open commitments” with “This meeting · review required” is a lower product context line. Full promise holds at least 2.6 seconds.
5. 12.30–15.90 — Ask Kesami. “What have I promised Acme?” immediately visible. Three cited historical promises arrive with meeting/date/timestamp references. Emphasize the Friday proposal and its exact quote. Query stays readable throughout. “Kesami remembers.” supports the result.
6. 15.90–20.00 — Follow through and final brand. Actions choices: Save local task, Draft follow-up, Schedule meeting. A local follow-up draft appears with review status; no send. By 17.20 the left brand lockup holds “Remember what you promised.”, Kesami, “AI meeting memory for macOS + Windows” and Get Kesami. Product UI remains at right through the end. CTA and final lockup hold about 2.8 seconds.

## Music cue guidance
Bundled Happy Beats / Business Moves vol. 12, ~109.96 BPM; optional strong cue locks at 8.74 seconds (hero focus) and 17.47 seconds (final brand). Card/reference accents can use 6.00, 6.56, 7.09, 7.64 seconds; natural reading holds take precedence. Music at a moderate low bed, gently lifted for hero and final reveal, faded in/out. Subtle precomputed RMS modulation on the warm orange accent rule only, never text. Sparse soft UI effects synchronized to the completed visual implementation.
