The film is a source-derived editorial UI reconstruction with fictional data, not a capture of a shipped cross-meeting commitment dashboard. “You committed to” and the enlarged card are cinematic copy and focus treatment. “3 open commitments” is scoped on screen to this meeting and backed by the accompanying synthetic fixture. The entity chain illustrates stored source references. It does not advertise a relationship graph interface or autonomous entity resolution.

The fictional Acme scenario adapts website/lib/demo.ts. Additional pending commitments exist only in demo-fixture.json for the per-meeting hero count. Ask's three concise rows are representative cited evidence, not an exhaustive promise report. Draft review is visible and its saved status explicitly says it was not sent. “Save local task” replaces a reminder notification capability the product does not implement.

Audio: bundled brag vol. 12 music and Kenney interface/soft-impact samples. Music is trimmed and faded to exactly 20 seconds, with understated mix automation. No narration. No customer transcripts, credentials, prompts or personal records were accessed.
